Desk gifts that do not end up in a drawer

The hard part of buying a desk gift is that you are buying for a desk you have not seen. Everything below either needs no measuring, or says plainly what it needs.

Desk gifts fail in two ways, and both are predictable. Either the person already owns one, or it does not fit the desk you never measured. A mat is the wrong width. A riser needs a monitor they do not have. A mug that was meant to be used sits in a cupboard.

So the list below is ordered by how much you know about the recipient. The first four work for a desk you have never seen. The next three assume you have. The last one is for someone who is leaving, which is its own problem - it has to still make sense at a desk neither of you has met.

What not to buy for someone else's desk

  1. A desk mat. It only works at the right width, and getting that wrong means it lives rolled up behind a cupboard. Buy one for your own desk, not theirs.
  2. A mechanical keyboard. Anyone who wants one already has opinions about switches. Anyone who does not will keep the keyboard they have.
  3. Anything with a quote on it. It has to survive their taste, their manager's taste, and the next office move. Very little does.
